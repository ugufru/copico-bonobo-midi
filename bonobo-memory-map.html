Bonobo v2.4: where the card lives in the CoCo's address space

From the CPU's point of view, the Bonobo has no ROM, no RAM and no registers. It decodes four addresses into four strobe wires to the Pico, and it can drive four control lines plus the data bus. Every "register" is just whatever the Pico does in real time during a strobe. Click any region, byte or signal to see the hardware path behind it.
